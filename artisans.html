The Parampara Sovereign Pledge

Transforming Craft Economics:
70% Direct Payout to Artisans

In conventional retail, traditional artisans receive less than 10-15% of the end retail price. Parampara eliminates middlemen, aggregators, and commercial markups. Every acquisition triggers a direct, auditable electronic settlement to the artisan's registered banking account.

Direct-to-Bank Telemetry: Live 12-digit UTR tracking on every order verifying instant artisan remittance.

GI Certified Provenance: Government Geographical Indication certificate embedded in every acquisition deed.

Living Apprenticeships: Direct funds sustain craft schools and youth apprentice stipends in heritage clusters.
