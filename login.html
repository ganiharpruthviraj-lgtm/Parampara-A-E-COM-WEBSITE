Collector Protocol

Terms of Service.

Governing authentic craft acquisitions from India's 28 heritage states.

1. GI Certified Authenticity Guarantee

Every item listed in the Parampara Collector Portal carries verifiable Geographical Indication (GI) accreditation or master artisan lineage. Counterfeit or mass-produced substitutes are strictly prohibited.

2. Direct 70% Artisan Proceeds

Parampara operates under a transparent fair-trade charter: a minimum of 70% of gross collector sales proceeds flow directly to the registered custodial artisan families.

3. Collector Archive Access

Collector accounts permit exclusive access to master craftsman archives, provenance deeds, and private showcase reservations across all 28 states.

4. Museum-Grade Preservation & Delivery

Acquired artifacts are packed in climate-controlled museum containers with bonded transit insurance from artisan workshops directly to your destination.

Data Confidentiality

Privacy Policy.

Your personal data and collection records are guarded under bank-grade encryption.

1. 256-Bit Encrypted Vault

All communications, authentication tokens, and purchase histories are encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 and TLS 1.3 standards.

2. Google OAuth Security

When signing in with Google, Parampara receives only your verified name and email address. We never inspect your private Google drive or sensitive account telemetry.

3. Zero Commercial Selling

We never sell, rent, or syndicate collector information to third-party ad exchanges or tracking conglomerates.

4. Complete Data Sovereignty

You may export your complete acquisition deed history or delete your collector profile permanently at any time by contacting our heritage desk.
