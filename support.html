Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about authentic Geographical Indication crafts and direct artisan trade.

What guarantees that an item on Parampara is authentic?
Every craft on Parampara is registered under India's official Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999. We work solely with master artisan guilds and national awardees. Each delivered item includes a tamper-proof provenance seal and a downloadable digital Certificate of Authenticity signed with GI Registry references.
How does the 70% direct artisan payout work?
Unlike conventional e-commerce platforms that take 40–60% markups, Parampara routes 70% of every rupee directly into the master artisan's cluster bank account via automated RazorpayX disbursements. The remaining 30% strictly covers raw materials provenance testing, insured speed delivery, and platform hosting.
How long does handcrafted creation and delivery take?
Readily completed heirloom pieces dispatch within 24 to 48 hours. Custom woven pit-loom textiles (such as Patan Patola or Kadwa Banarasi) require 14 to 30 days of handloom dedication. Once dispatched, India Post Speed Post reaches metro addresses in 2–4 business days and remote Himalayan/Northeast circles in 5–7 days.
Can I track my shipment live with India Post Speed Post?
Yes! Upon artisan cluster dispatch, an official Speed Post AWB number (IP + 9 digits + IN) is generated. You can paste your AWB code into our dedicated Parcel Tracking Portal to view every sorting hub checkpoint in real time.
What is the return or replacement policy for fragile handicrafts?
We provide a 7-Day Heritage Exchange Guarantee. If your craft arrives damaged in transit or differs from its GI provenance certificate, upload an unboxing video or photo within 7 days in your Collector Profile to receive an immediate replacement or full refund.

7-Day Free Replacement Policy

Because authentic GI crafts are handcrafted without industrial factory molds, slight natural nuances in wood grain, silk slubs, or mineral dye gradients are hallmarks of human artistry. However, if an item arrives damaged, cracked, or inconsistent with its certification, we provide an unconditional replacement or refund within 7 days of delivery.

Simple 3-Step Claim Process:
  1. Open your Collector Profile and locate the delivered order.
  2. Click "Request Return / Support" and attach a clear photo of the transit damage or flaw.
  3. Our concierge schedules a reverse India Post pickup from your doorstep and initiates a full refund or replacement.

Refund Processing Timeline

Refunds are credited directly to your original payment source (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or Net Banking) within 3 to 5 business days via Razorpay. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are transferred instantly via NEFT/UPI after customer confirmation.

Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Ethical guidelines governing our digital craft preservation platform.

1. Provenance Integrity & GI Act Compliance

All products listed on Parampara are verified in accordance with the Geographical Indications of Goods Act, 1999. Unauthorized replica manufacturers or powerloom impersonators are legally barred from our network.

2. Direct Artisan Payout Covenant

By purchasing through Parampara, you participate in a legally binding fair-trade compact: a minimum of 70% of product price is earmarked and settled directly to the artisan's guild bank account, eliminating middlemen exploitation.

3. Data Privacy & Collector Protection

We strictly protect collector contact and payment information under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal details. Payment transactions are processed with 256-bit encryption by Razorpay; Parampara never stores full card details on its servers.
